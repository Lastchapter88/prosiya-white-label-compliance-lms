## Security services

ProSiya provides operational security services, including:

1. **Physical Guarding** — On-site security personnel to protect properties and assets.
2. **Loss Prevention** — Strategies and measures to prevent theft and reduce losses.
3. **VIP Protection** — Specialised close protection for high-profile individuals.
4. **Mobile Patrols** — Vehicle patrols to deter incidents and respond quickly.
5. **Private Investigation** — Discreet investigations to gather information and evidence.
6. **Special Events** — Event security tailored to keep guests safe and operations orderly.
7. **Escorting** — Safe transport for people or valuables.
8. **Armed Response** — Rapid armed response to security threats and alarms.

These services can support commercial properties, warehouses, factories, construction sites, events, and other business security needs when appropriate — exact suitability depends on the site and should be confirmed with the ProSiya team.
